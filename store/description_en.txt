[Title]
zzClockPlus - Clock, Temperature, GPS, Battery

[Description]
A clock data field with a status row: temperature, GPS signal and battery level above a big clock.
Built with the TitilliumWeb font for better readability on the move.
A rebuilt version of zzClock.

[Clock]
- One slot of the 10-field layout: hours and minutes. Wide slots: hours, minutes and seconds.
- 12-hour, 24-hour or follow the device setting. AM/PM shown in blue.

[Status row]
- Temperature from the device sensor, in °C or °F (follow device or choose).
- GPS signal bars: orange bars for signal strength, gray slashed icon when there is no signal.
- Battery % and icon: green, yellow under 30%, red under 15%, and a charging icon.
- Turn off "Show units" to hide C, % and AM/PM and make the status row bigger.

[Alerts]
- GPS: if there is no GPS signal in the first 30 seconds after you start or resume recording, the background flashes red.
- Parts mileage: after each activity the distance is added to up to 6 parts (chain, tires...). If any part is past its replacement distance, the background turns blue for 30 seconds after you start.

[Supported devices]
Edge 540 / 550 / 840 / 850 / 1040 / 1050 / 1060

Change settings in the Garmin Connect IQ app or Garmin Express.
